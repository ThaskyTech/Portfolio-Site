// ABOUT

Hi, I'm Thabang

I'm a final-year Software Engineering student at Belgium Campus iTversity in Pretoria, and I'm aiming my career at robotics and intelligent systems: writing the software that makes small machines see, decide and move.

That started with backends: Express APIs, databases and authentication. It grew into hardware when I began building a litter-collecting robot with a friend, and then a vision-guided servo arm. Now I work across Arduino and Raspberry Pi, Fusion 360 and machine learning, and I'm digging deeper into algorithms and data pipelines.

I lead the Robotics Club at my campus. I'm looking for roles that mix software with hardware, and in the meantime I take on freelance projects.
