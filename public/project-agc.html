Autonomous Garbage Collector

A self-driving vehicle that finds and picks up litter on its own, built to tackle litter in South African communities.

STATUSIn progress, since 2025
TEAMBuilt with a friend
STACKPython, TensorFlow, OpenCV, Raspberry Pi
CONTEXTGrew out of the Robotics Club

The idea

A vehicle with a six-wheel omnidirectional drive, a robotic arm for pickup, an onboard trash compartment and a sensor suite for object detection. A companion scout drone is planned for later.

Three iterations

  • Star 1: proof of concept.
  • Star 2: scaled hardware and software validation.
  • Star 3: full-scale, deployment-ready unit.

Challenges

Training and validating the detection model with limited datasets, and getting the arm to coordinate with objects that lie at different orientations.

What I've built so far

Object detection demo models and STL designs for the robotic arm, both on my GitHub. The main takeaway so far is how much of robotics is coordinating software with hardware.
